# Assessor sheet — draft, human review required

Use only after following PILOT_CONTRACT.md consent, allocation and stopping rules. These are authored evaluation examples, not observed participant answers. Do not show the transfer items during training.

Baseline and day 12: show the 12 Food & Drink targets from the unchanged seed in independently shuffled order. Ask for English meanings without choices, hints or dictionary. One point per correct meaning; accept ordinary synonyms. Water's grammatical gender is not required. Use participant IDs only.

Transfer: ask the learner to identify the requested food/drink on each unfamiliar snippet; score one point each for the specified meaning, accepting synonyms. Do not require knowledge of non-target words.

1. `Sandwich au fromage — 6 €` — What food is named after “au”? **Cheese**.
2. `Poisson et légumes — 14 €` — Name the two foods. **Fish and vegetables** (both required).
3. `Café ou eau ?` — What two drinks are offered? **Coffee or water** (both).
4. `Poulet, pain, beurre` — Which word names chicken? **Poulet**.
5. `Fruits — 3 €` — What food is priced? **Fruit**.
6. `Vin — 5 € / Café — 2 €` — Which drink costs 5 euros? **Wine**.

These test recognition in new text, not speaking or authentic travel competence. Small sets can overestimate learning. Record pre-known words and alternate exposure; a native/proficient reviewer must approve the wording/rubric before use. A replication should include harder independently authored tasks and a credible active-recall baseline.
